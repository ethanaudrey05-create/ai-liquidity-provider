# Privacy Policy - AI Liquidity Provider

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "AI Liquidity Provider" ("the Connector"), operated by Independent AI Developer ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Connector through Muse.

## 1. What the Connector does
The Connector helps users monitor liquidity positions on Solana (including Orca), analyze portfolio risk, screen new tokens, and prepare actions to open or close positions. It is non-custodial. We never hold, control, or have access to your funds. Every open or close action requires your explicit confirmation and your signature in your own wallet. Nothing is executed automatically.

## 2. Information we handle
- Public wallet addresses that you provide, used to read public blockchain data.
- Requests and parameters you send through Muse, such as token addresses, pool names, position sizes, and slippage settings.
- Transaction details prepared for your review and the resulting public transaction signature, if you choose to sign.
- Basic technical logs, such as timestamps, request types, and error messages.

## 3. Information we do not collect
We never ask for or store private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, or signing permissions. Please never share them with the Connector. We do not collect your name, government ID, or financial account details through the Connector.

## 4. How we use information
- To return the data and analysis you request.
- To prepare transactions for your review and confirmation.
- To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Connector.
- To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse.
- To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 5. Third-party services
The Connector may retrieve or send data to third parties, including blockchain RPC providers, decentralized exchanges and protocols (such as Orca), market data providers, and your wallet provider. Public blockchain data is visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us. These third parties have their own privacy practices. Muse is operated by its own provider and is subject to its own privacy policy.

## 6. Sharing of information
We do not share your information except with the third-party services described above (only as needed), when required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the Connector.

## 7. Data retention
Technical logs are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted or anonymized. Information recorded on a public blockchain is permanent and outside our control.

## 8. Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we handle. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Because the Connector is non-custodial, you are responsible for protecting your wallet and signing only transactions you understand.

## 9. Your choices and rights
You may stop using the Connector at any time. You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion of data associated with you.

## 10. Children
The Connector is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 11. Not financial advice
Information provided by the Connector is for informational purposes only and is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Crypto assets and liquidity provision carry a high risk of loss.

## 12. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised.
